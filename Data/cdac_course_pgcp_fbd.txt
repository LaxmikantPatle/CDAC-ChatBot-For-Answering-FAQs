PGCP-FBD will equip students with the skill set to completely decode the FinTech and 
Blockchain puzzle, imparting fundamental clarity on the required components, and will 
provide a strong understanding of the Blockchain and FinTech technologies involved along 
with their applications/business as is being seen in the current scenario. The course will 
systematically take candidates through the latest technologies of Blockchain, FinTech 
development, Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning (AI/ML) in FinTech and Policy, 
Risks and standards associated with it that are becoming the hallmark of advanced business 
applications.

The educational eligibility criteria for PGCP-FBD course is

Graduate in Engineering or Technology (10+2+4 or 10+3+3 years) in IT / Computer Science / Electronics / Telecommunications / Electrical / Instrumentation, OR
MSc/MS (10+2+3+2 years) in Computer Science, IT, Electronics OR



Graduate in any Discipline Engineering (10+2+4 or 10+3+3 years) . OR
MCA, / MCM, OR 
Post Graduate Degree in Physics / Mathematics / Statistics
The candidate must have 55% in the qualifying degree.
